Resource Profile: PH eReferral PractitionerRole

Official URL: https://fhir.doh.gov.ph/pheref/StructureDefinition/ereferral-practitioner-role Version: 0.1.0
Draft as of 2026-09-09 Computable Name: ERefPractitionerRole

Profile on PractitionerRole for the Philippines eReferral specification, extending PHCorePractitionerRole. This profile captures the role of the referring practitioner and care navigator within the eReferral workflow, linking practitioners to healthcare facilities.

Usages:

  • Refer to this Profile: EReferral Provenance, EReferral ServiceRequest and EReferral Task
  • Examples for this Profile: PractitionerRole/ExampleERefPractitionerRoleReceiving and PractitionerRole/ExampleERefPractitionerRoleSubmission

You can also check for usages in the FHIR IG Statistics
